## What affects the price

- **Crane size**: bigger tonnage costs more per day.
- **Rental length**: weekly and monthly hire lowers the daily rate.
- **Operator and riggers**: included or charged separately.
- **Site access and permits**: road closures or night work add cost.

## Typical price ranges

| Crane | Daily rate (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| 25 – 50 ton mobile crane | 1,500 – 2,500 |
| 70 – 100 ton mobile crane | 2,800 – 4,500 |
| 200 ton and above | On request |
