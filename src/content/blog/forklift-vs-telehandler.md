## The short answer

A forklift is best on flat, hard ground for lifting and moving pallets. A telehandler
is better on rough sites and when you need to reach up and forward.

## Side-by-side

| Feature | Forklift | Telehandler |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reach | Straight up | Up and forward |
| Terrain | Flat, hard floors | Rough ground |
| Typical use | Warehouses, ports | Construction sites |
